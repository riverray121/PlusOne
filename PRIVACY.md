# PlusOne Privacy Policy

PlusOne does not collect or sell personal data, and it has no servers,
accounts, analytics, or advertising.

- **Camera.** The unlock selfie is analyzed on your device, in memory, using
  Apple's Vision framework. Frames are never saved, never leave your device,
  and are discarded immediately after the face check.
- **Blocked apps.** Your choice of blocked apps and websites is handled by
  Apple's Screen Time framework, which gives PlusOne only opaque tokens. The
  app cannot see which apps you selected, and this data stays on your device.
- **Friend approvals.** If you pair with a friend to approve settings
  changes, that pairing and its approval requests sync through your personal
  iCloud account via Apple's CloudKit service. This data is visible only to
  you and the paired friend, and PlusOne's developer has no access to it.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/riverray121/PlusOne/issues.
